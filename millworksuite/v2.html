§02Our Approach
Manifesto

The goal is not to replace estimators or draftsmen. It's to let them work at their best.

Skilled people are scarce in this trade. The bottleneck isn't talent — it's the volume of repetitive work piled in front of it. MillworkSuite is built around a single principle: amplify expertise, eliminate repetition.

A Time Problem, Not a Skill Problem

Estimators don't bid slowly because they aren't good at it. They bid slowly because the work is tedious and time consuming.

Marking up cabinets in a PDF isn't expertise. Copying counts into a spreadsheet isn't expertise. Re-keying the same project into Microvellum isn't expertise. It's transcription dressed up as estimation or drafting.

The actual hard work — reading the room, anticipating field conditions, sequencing the install, optimizing the cost approach, catching what the architect didn't draw — is what your team is paid for. That's where their judgment shows up. That's where margin is won or lost.

MillworkSuite handles the volume so the people in your shop can spend their time on the work that requires their attention.

§04Revisions
Revision-aware

Architects revise. Your scope shouldn't restart.

Drawings don't arrive once. Architects deliver a base set, then an addendum, then post-award revisions, change directives, etc. MillworkSuite handles every version — without rebuilding the job from scratch.

Diff revisions. Update only what changed. Push deltas to CAD.

Upload a new drawing set. MillworkSuite compares it page-by-page, elevation-by-elevation against the prior version. Added cabinets, removed cabinets, dimension changes, finish swaps — every difference is flagged and ready for review.

  • + Detect Page-level diffing. Match revised pages to the original set
  • ~ Reprice Re-price only what changed. The unchanged scope remains. Update your bid within minutes — not days.
  • → Push Export the revised job. Microvellum and Cabinet Vision get the updated project — reimport only what changed.

§05How It Works

Five steps. End to end.

One product, multiple drawing versions, one source of truth. From the architect's PDF to the draftsman's open project — without re-entry, without spreadsheets, without losing days of estimating effort.

01
Drawing Intake
Upload architectural PDFs. MillworkSuite auto-classifies every page by room and elevation type.
02
AI Scope Discovery
The AI engine detects every casework item — base cabinets, uppers, countertops, soffits, etc. — across every elevation. Verified at 90%+ accuracy on unseen architect styles.
03
Pricing Engine
Apply your company's pricing catalog. The engine maps detected items to your SKUs, applies unit pricing, and calculates a complete priced scope — with a user-review step before the proposal is locked.

Export a complete, formatted scope of work and quote to Excel. Client-ready using your company branding.
05
CAD Export
Push the approved scope directly to Microvellum or Cabinet Vision using your existing CAD Libraries. Drafting starts with live CAD files of cabinets — not a blank screen.
