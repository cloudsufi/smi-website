🎯 1 — Brand Essence & Voice

Mission Statement

✦
To eliminate the manual burden of millwork estimating through purpose-built AI — so that firms can bid faster, estimate more accurately, and spend more time doing what they were hired to do: building exceptional millwork.

The Manifesto

Every day, skilled millwork estimators sit down and do a job that machines should be doing. They sort three-hundred-page PDFs by hand. They hunt elevation callouts across dozens of sheets. They transcribe measurements. They retype scope items into spreadsheets, copy-paste proposals into Word, re-enter cabinet layouts into CAD. Not because they want to — but because until now, they had to.
🏆
One platform. One session. Zero re-entry.
MillworkSuite is the answer to that waste — a system built from the ground up for this industry, this workflow, and these professionals.

Core Values

ValueWhat It Means in Practice
Precision over speedFast is only valuable when accurate. Never sacrifice correctness for velocity.
Industry specificityEvery word, every feature, every design choice is calibrated for millwork — not construction in general.
Automation as empowermentAI frees skilled estimators to exercise expertise rather than perform data entry.
Transparency in workflowShow value, don't just claim it. The Before/After comparison and workflow steps are central to this.
Quiet confidenceDeclarative statements. No hedging. Dark, restrained palette signals a brand secure in its capabilities.

Brand Personality

Expert Direct Technical but Accessible Quietly Confident

Voice & Tone by Context

ContextTone
Marketing headlinesBold, declarative, problem-aware. Max 8–10 words. Imperative-first.
Feature descriptionsClinical, precise, benefit-focused. Lead with the outcome.
CTA copyAction-oriented, no-friction. "Schedule a Demo." Never "Learn More."
Error / edge casesClear, calm, actionable. Never blame the user.
Social / LinkedInExpert, industry-insider. Share workflow insights, not product-first promo.
⚠️
Language to avoid: "World-class", "best-in-class", "innovative solution", "game-changing". These are placeholders. Use specifics: quantities, time comparisons, named integrations.

🔷 2 — Logo & Identity

Logotype Construction

The wordmark is set in Syne — geometric, high-contrast display sans-serif. Styled as a compound noun: Millwork in primary text color, Suite in Precision Green (#28C873).

💡
The chromatic split segments the two conceptual components: the industry domain (Millwork) and the product category (Suite). It also creates a distinctive recall trigger — even at peripheral vision the green syllable is immediately identifiable.

Do's & Don'ts

✓ Do

  • Place on dark backgrounds (primary)
  • Maintain exact proportions always

Three Visual Signature Elements

Hero Grid — 60×60px orthographic pattern

A precise 60×60px orthographic grid rendered as 1px lines in rgba(40,200,115,0.07). Masked radially — visible at center, fading to transparent at edges. References architectural drawing paper and engineering notation. Must always use the radial mask — never at full opacity.

Radial Glow Gradients — ambient AI energy

Elliptical radial gradients in Precision Green at 7–18% opacity in hero sections, CTAs, and workflow backgrounds. Creates a bioluminescent ambient light effect. Must be elliptical, centered on/above primary content, never exceeding 18% opacity at core.

SVG Film Grain — material presence

Fixed-position fractalNoise SVG overlay at 3% opacity applied as a body ::after pseudo-element. Prevents flat digital surfaces from feeling antiseptic. Must be imperceptible at normal viewing distances — experienced as material quality, not visible as a pattern. pointer-events: none.

Photography Direction

✓ Use
  • Workshop interiors and drafting environments
  • Close-up hands with precision tools
  • Architectural drawings / blueprint imagery
  • Manufacturing facility details
✗ Avoid
  • Generic office stock with laptops
  • Smiling professionals posed for camera
  • Aspirational lifestyle imagery
  • Construction sites without millwork focus

🧠 9 — Psychology & Strategy

The 5-Step Conversion Sequence

1
Credibility
Is this legitimate? — Dark environment, serif headlines, film grain, precise icon system answer "yes" in under 500ms.
2
Relevance
Is this for me? — "Stop estimating manually", "millwork and casework", the specific pain list answer within 8–12 seconds.
3
Desire
Do I want this? — Before/After comparison + 6-step workflow + integration details build concrete desire.
4
Trust
Can I trust this? — LinkedIn badge, Orlando identity, transparent demo CTA, specific product descriptions.
5
Action
What should I do? — "Schedule a Demo" in the fixed nav + hero ensures the CTA is never more than one scroll away.

Gestalt Principles Applied

PrincipleWhere AppliedEffect
Figure/Ground#28C873 CTAs on near-black backgrounds (~23:1 contrast)CTA is always unambiguously the figure. Next action is always self-evident.
ProximityFeature grid — 1px inter-card spacing"These belong together" — a complete system, not disparate features.
SimilarityConsistent icon stroke weight, color, and container across all sectionsVisual language decoded on first encounter. No "what kind of element is this?" questions.
ContinuationWorkflow section connector lineEye follows step 1 to step 6 as a smooth, linear, effortless progression.
ClosureBefore/After comparison — "Old Way" card is deliberately incompleteThe eye seeks closure and finds it in the MillworkSuite card's triumphant summary.

Color Psychology Summary

🟢
Why not blue? Blue is the default B2B SaaS color — Salesforce, Microsoft, Jira. By using green as the sole accent, MillworkSuite creates immediate visual distinction in any competitive set while leveraging associations with "go," "approved," "profit," and financial growth.
⬛
Why dark mode? The deep forest blacks (with subtle green cast — #060D08 is not neutral black, it's dark green) create infinite depth and resonate subliminally with "millwork" — a craft rooted in natural wood and forest materials. Premium tools, precision instruments, and professional software all default to dark environments.

Memorability Strategy

The most memorable brands own a single, distinctive visual element. For MillworkSuite, that element is #28C873 Precision Green on deep black.

Consistent application High-priority contexts only Distinctive hue (not standard web green) ≠ #00FF00 web green ≠ #00C853 material green #28C873 MillworkSuite green
